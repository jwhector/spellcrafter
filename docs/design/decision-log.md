# The Hollow: decision log

What was decided, what it replaced, and where the rule lives now, newest first.
The docs show only the current design (Decided Nov 5); this log is where the
history goes. "Frozen" names the Claude Doc the old wording is in, at the
revision listed in the [README](README.md#where-the-docs-came-from); passages
that were cut word for word are also in the [archive](README.md#the-archive).

A rule that is still Proposed or Open is in the docs, not here. A row here is
something Jared decided.

## Nov 8

| Decision | What it replaced | Where it lives now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The game has ten runes, in five opposed pairs: Fire and Frost, Life and Death, Stone and Air, Revelation and Illusion, Space and Time (Jared's names). What each new rune does is Open. | 16 runes in seven opposite pairs plus Portal and Stone (Nov 5) | [System C rules](system-c.md#the-ten-runes) |
| The Time rune is renamed Temporal (Jared's name). | Time (earlier on Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#temporal) |
| The Space rune is renamed Spatial (Jared's name). | Space (earlier on Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#the-ten-runes) |
| Where today's runes go: Life takes in Mend, Death takes in Wound, Space takes in Portal, and Time takes in Haste and Slow. Revelation takes in Dispel and Expose. Illusion takes in Invisibility, Amplification and Diminishing. Stone takes in protection (Protect), and Air takes in pushing and pulling units, the job planned for Blank; whether a Blank rune stays alongside the ten is Open. | Each of those as a rune of its own | [System C rules](system-c.md#the-ten-runes) |
| There is no Summon rune. A summon is found as a combination of runes. | Summon as a rune whose center makes a summon (Nov 4) | [System C rules](system-c.md#recipes) |
| System C: a third crafting system, documented beside A and B. Spells are discovered recipes of up to three runes, crafted with minor runes, and there are no centers. Discovery is discontinuous; crafting is continuous. | System B's center and ring as the only freeform system | [System C rules](system-c.md) |
| In System C every recipe shape has a job: A is the rune's basic spell, AA and AAA its school's archetype scaled up, AB a spell of two schools, AAB the AB spell leaning toward A (and ABB toward B), and ABC a unique mechanic whose uniqueness is its power. | The same jobs, marked Proposed (Nov 7) | [System C rules](system-c.md#recipes) |
| In System C, each minor rune is a table with one effect for every output kind. A spell exposes its outputs, and the player chooses which one each minor rune changes. | A minor rune modifying a center's major output | [System C rules](system-c.md#minor-runes) |
| Minor runes only transform, never add (Nov 7), and each should set up plays with other spells, players or the board. | Minor rune effects that added an effect, such as a weak heal | [System C rules](system-c.md#minor-runes) |
| Stat dials (a bonus-action cast, a shorter cooldown, a stronger effect) are not minor runes; they move to a dial of their own. | Haste's quick cast, Diminishing's cooldown and Amplification's "a third stronger" as minor rune effects | [System C rules](system-c.md#minor-runes) |
| Randomness is welcome, except in accuracy for now; charm and domination (a unit switching sides) are acceptable. | Direct control as something that "may come later as a rare, expensive effect" (Nov 4) | [Overview](overview.md#battles) |
| Air pushes or pulls, chosen when you craft, and System C has no Push and Pull inscriptions. | Push and Pull as inscriptions (System B keeps them) | [System C rules](system-c.md#air) |
| As far as possible, a recipe with more runes gives more options, not more power. | Nothing: a three-rune spell risked being strictly better than its pairs and singles (Jared's concern, Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#recipes) |
| Every minor rune has an empowered effect, got by spending that rune from the air. Recipe runes fill the air (one rune per spell, chosen when it is cast) and minor runes spend it. The air is shared with enemies, anyone can spend, including whoever filled it, and spending is a choice. An empowered effect takes the rune's transform one step further, one rule per rune to start, with exceptions allowed; on an output that lasts, it holds only for the round it is cast. | Nothing: minor runes had one effect per output | [System C rules](system-c.md#empowered-effects) |
| Air keeps sliding a spell's area until it meets something, as its empowered cell on a spell; Frost gets a different cell. | System B's Frost on a spell (Nov 5), which System B keeps | [System C rules](system-c.md#air) |
| Illusion hides what a spell is: the other side sees that it's an illusion, where it is and its output kind, not what it does, until it's revealed. When you cast, you choose whether it looks threatening or beneficial (for now), and the look may lie. Empowered, it shows a decoy footprint as well. | Illusion's minor idea "unseen", carried over from Invisibility | [System C rules](system-c.md#illusion) |
| Players learn an enemy's spells only as they're cast or shown. | Nothing: it was unstated | [Overview](overview.md#battles) |
| Spatial is about where a spell comes from: a spell that can reach an ally carries on from them, with its range counted again. Empowered, it opens a one-way spell portal anyone can use for the round. Spells can't travel through a gate unless a minor rune lets them. Spatial's cells are set (all but the empowered cell on a spell). | Spatial's minor idea "from elsewhere", carried over from Portal, and swapping places, which was turned down as its core | [System C rules](system-c.md#spatial) |
| Each cell of a rune's row, empowered cells included, can be its own effect, as long as it fits the rune and works on any spell with that output kind. | One empowered rule per rune for all its cells, to start (earlier on Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#empowered-effects) |
| Stone raises stone you can climb: it doesn't block movement, blocks line of sight, stops a shove, gives high ground, and a unit pushed off it falls. Walls that block movement are kept for authored spells. Stone's cells are set (all but the cell on a spell), and every summon needs a ridden animation. | Stone's minor ideas "anchors" (now its ground cell) and "a shield from what the spell did", and raising full walls | [System C rules](system-c.md#stone) |
| Temporal readies a spell: it waits, shown to everyone, and goes off the first time a condition chosen when you craft is met in the enemy phase, or at the start of next round if nothing sets it off. Empowered, it stays ready through the next round's enemy phase too. Tempo is kept for an authored spell with a cooldown, and rewind is noted as an authored spell idea. | Haste and Slow's drawn-out idea, carried over as Time's minor idea | [System C rules](system-c.md#temporal) |
| Temporal's cells: a gate open only in the players' phase; a summon that holds its action (empowered, a second one); a shield that goes to the first ally attacked (empowered, moves on after each hit); ground active in only one phase for its first round, chosen when crafting (empowered, for as long as the ground lasts, an exception to the one-round rule for lasting outputs); on the whole battlefield, players hold their bonus action (empowered, their action). On a spell, the enemy's next spell is held where it was aimed and lands at the start of next round, shown to everyone. Worked out assuming enemies don't show their next move. | Going last in the enemy phase, for the cell on a spell (turned down: enemies act together) | [System C rules](system-c.md#temporal) |
| Fire is contagion: what a Fire spell leaves on a unit spreads, and each recipe spell names what it passes on. On ground, it spreads 1 hex in every direction at the end of the round, and empowered once more. | Spreads: next round the effect hits again on neighbours at half strength (System B, Nov 5, which System B keeps) | [System C rules](system-c.md#fire) |
| Fire's contagion is passed around: it passes only when its carrier moves next to a unit or is pushed into one, leaving the carrier, and it grows only under certain conditions, being displaced among them. | Contagion that copied to each unit it touched (earlier on Nov 8): Jared worried it was duplication for free | [System C rules](system-c.md#fire) |
| A minor rune's game stands on its own: it never needs particular runes in the recipe, an authored spell is complete without any minor rune, and some authored spells may touch a minor rune's game on purpose. | Nothing: it was unstated | [System C rules](system-c.md#minor-runes) |
| Fire on ground: a unit pushed out of it drags it along every hex it crosses, and the end-of-round spread becomes the empowered cell. Fire through a gate, for now: a burst, so everything within 1 hex of a traveller goes too, and 2 hexes empowered. | Fire on ground spreading 1 hex at the end of the round as the base (earlier on Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#fire) |
| Life takes root: the spell grows while its caster stays put, up to double, and moving or being moved resets it; what can't grow stronger grows longer. Its cells are set. | Spares allies (System B, Nov 5, which System B keeps) | [System C rules](system-c.md#life) |
| An empowered cell is value in itself. Units carried along by Fire's gate burst don't burst again. | Nothing: both were unstated | [Empowered effects](system-c.md#empowered-effects), [Fire](system-c.md#fire) |
| Two more output kinds: along the caster's path, and on a link between two units. | Seven kinds, with a link added only when an authored spell needed it (earlier on Nov 8) | [System C rules](system-c.md#outputs) |
