Spell audit

Compiles every circle in the chosen scope with the same grammar as the circle grammar map, merges circles that are the same spell, and sorts what's left into lenses worth a look. Since Nov 4 it includes the three new rune pairs (amplification and diminishing, haste and slow, invisibility and revelation; named by Jared Nov 5), the on-ground column, up to three extra points, the new inscriptions and triggers, and the single-target bonus. Pick any spell to read its case file and mark it fine, a bug, or a design question. The value numbers come from a rough, editable price list, so treat them as a way to sort spells, not as balance.

Value model (rough, editable)

Each kind of effect has a price per unit touched (terrain is priced per hex). The center counts in full and each ring rune at half. Modifiers: expose with damage ×1.4 on the damage, movers with hazard terrain add a combo bonus, summon traits and global rules repeat (×2), lingering ×1.6 on ground, things and global rules and ×1.3 on statuses or a spell, steerable ×1.2, follows the unit ×1.15, wound's extra damage below half health, a single-target bonus ×1.15 on single, bolt and self (decided Nov 4), ground costs 25% of what it spreads because filters can't narrow it, a delay ×0.8, a trigger ×0.75, friendly fire costs 35% of the damage or help spread on an area, and cancelling pairs count as zero. Value per slot is the raw value divided by the circle's slots. The forge would scale real numbers to fit the budget, so high value per slot means the price list undervalues that spell, which is exactly what to look at.
